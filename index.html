About Infinity Entertainment

We are two friends from the Czech Republic and we just love making games. We put everything into our projects, and our dream is to make this our full-time job so we can spend all our time building great experiences for you :)

F

František Svoboda

Programming & Game Design

Hi, I'm František, Founder & CEO of Infinity Entertainment. I've loved games my whole life, and that passion eventually led me to start making them myself. Building them takes a ton of work, but the absolute best part is just watching people play and genuinely enjoy what we've created.

T

Tobiáš Štefl

3D Art & Game Design

Hi, I'm Tobiáš, Over time many games inspired me to try something using Blender. Then we started few projects, We realized that failures teach us the most, but we never gave up and we are still going for the game we dreamed of.
